**Status indicators:** ❌ not done, ➕ done, 〰 partially done

## Engagement System ➕

Actions are uninterruptible hour-long states. Resolution happens at hour-end.

### Time Flow

- `on_hour(N)`: Scheduled actions trigger, entities move and initiate engagements
- `[10-sec ticks]`: Movement continues, encounter checks can change target
- `on_hour_end(N)`: Engagements RESOLVE (damage dealt, blessings stolen, etc.)
- `on_hour(N+1)`: Next scheduled actions begin

### Entity-Specific Engagement Rules

#### Bandit

- Initiates `ROBBERY` on caravans
- Initiates `COMBAT` on heroes
- Flees from dragons

#### Hero

- Can mitigate robberies/attacks by joining the engagement
- **Vengeful:** kills bandits at resolution
- **Tired:** loses to dragons at resolution (unless in settlement)

#### Dragon

- Good dragons mitigate robberies/attacks on humans by joining the engagement
- Territorial dragons initiate `COMBAT` when near domain
- Dragons circle their target during the engagement

## User Interaction 〰

- Users can submit a form that defines some characteristics of a dragon 〰
- Users can submit the form up to as many times per day as there are active spires ❌
- If no active spires: the world carries on, but no user interaction is possible ❌
- If 5 spires are active for 5 consecutive days: the world stabilizes enough to always allow at least one dragon to be spawned ❌
- Dragons have **type**: serpent, blade, brute, midas, druid, fragile 〰
- Dragons have **diet**: herbivore, carnivore, anthropophage, greed ➕
- Dragons spawn at a chosen location that determines their color: green for plains, blue for water, grey for mountains, red for scorched ➕
- Dragons have **character**: neutral, good, evil, or territorial; if omitted, select at random favoring neutral 〰
- Dragons have **pronouns**; if omitted, use it/it/its ➕

## Update Loop ➕

- There is a daily update that determines settlement events and entity moods ➕
- After all moods are set, entities create a schedule for the day, evenly spreading the planned actions ➕
- There is an update every 10 seconds which processes the reactions and motions of the schedule (dragon circles the target, bandit stops the caravan, etc.) ➕

## Mobile Entities ➕

- Movement logic (state machine: `created→moving→arrived`) ➕
- Diagonal movement penalty (sqrt(2)-1 debt accumulation, skip turn when >=1) ➕
- Pathfinding (A* with Euclidean heuristic, diagonal cost sqrt(2)) ➕

## Thinking Entities 〰

- Intent property ➕
- Thoughts list (append via `think()`, max 20 kept) ➕
- Action/intent/idle thought generation (random every 10-30 cycles based on state/intent) ➕
- **Gossip and news** ❌
  - Caravans share news of dragons and bandits
  - Heroes share news of dragon lairs and grudges
  - Settlements exchange gossip of upcoming events and stored blessings

## Entity Types 〰

### Spirits and Cattle ➕

- Spirits spawn during generation at the most central tiles of forests, mountains and lakes ➕
- Spirits tended by a dragon receive a blessing ➕
- Cattle spawn every 50 cycles, max 20 in world, random plains location ➕
- Cattle wander randomly, gravitate toward villages within 10 tiles ➕
- Cattle fear dragons and scorched land ➕

### Settlements 〰

- Camps have 2 hit points, villages 3, cities 5 ❌
- Ruined cities and villages leave a ruin that produces a single bandit pack after two days ❌
- Ruined settlements disappear after 50 days (`Ruins` mixin) ➕
- Ruined worker camps disappear (via `Mortal` mixin) ➕
- Cities and villages have a rotation of daily events ➕

#### Settlements - Event: None 〰

- If village: may occasionally spawn a hero ➕
- If city: may occasionally heal ➕

#### Settlements - Event: Mourning 〰

- If not protected: occurs after being attacked by a dragon ➕
- Rarely occurs naturally ➕
- Doesn't send caravans ➕
- If village: 50% chance to spawn a hero
- Only one hero can be spawned this way

#### Settlements - Event: Market Day ➕

- Occurs every five days, unless mourning is forced ➕
- Attracts heroes ➕
- Neighbouring settlements may send a caravan ❌
- If city: may occasionally spawn a hero ➕

#### Settlements - Event: The Repairs 〰

- Occurs on the next day after market day if not full hit points, unless mourning is forced ➕
- Restores a hit point; double that if a hero rests in the settlement ❌

#### Settlements - Event: Celebration ❌

- Occurs on the next day after a blessing is brought in
- Can't happen again for another 5 days
- Same as market day, but overrides mourning and guarantees the hero spawn from a city

#### Settlements - General Features

- Cities and villages may send several caravans to nearby settlements; the closer the settlement, the higher the probability
- Worker camps take blessings from nearby spirits 1 at a time ➕
- Worker camps send blessings to their parent settlements the next day after getting one ➕
- Cities that accumulated 10 blessings create a spire on a day after a market day ➕
- Villages and cities send a settler caravan once every 2 days ➕
- Villages are limited to 4 camps (at least one wood); cities are limited to 6 (at least two ore). Requirement is ignored if there are no available spots left ➕
- Villages and cities can also create a camp by a lake that's not counted towards the total ❌
- A village can occupy a lake without a camp if it's close enough ➕
- When a city receives gossip about a blessing in a village, it sends a caravan to buy it ❌
- Cities with spires sell blessings to nearby cities ➕
- Ruins hold any blessings the settlement had and persist for a set amount of time ➕
- Ruins can be pillaged for blessings by heroes and bandits ➕

### Spire 〰

- Gold tower "Ї" ("1" if ruined), single tile near city ➕
- Spires age and crumble after 100 days (`Aging` mixin) ➕
- Spire ruins disappear after 50 days (`Ruins` mixin) ➕
- Destroyed along with the city ❌

### Caravans ➕

- If sent to trade: approach settlement, exchange gossip, return home ➕
- If sent to retrieve a blessing: approach village, buy the blessing, return home ➕
- If sent to deliver a blessing: approach camp's parent settlement ➕
- If sent to settle camp: pathfind to location, create camp ➕
- Flee to nearest settlement if target dies ➕

### Bandits 〰

- Age and die after 50 days (`Aging` mixin) ➕
- Don't drop trinkets when dying of old age ➕
- Alternate daily between hiding in forests to ambush caravans, and seeking lairs, treasuries and ruins to pillage 〰
- When attacking caravans: halt delivery, steal blessing ➕
- Attack nearby villages if haven't robbed or pillaged in 3 days ➕
- Carry up to 3 stolen blessings as trinkets until death ➕
- If stealing blessings when already having 3, the newly stolen blessings are permanently lost ❌
- Fear dragons ➕

### Heroes 〰

- If spawned from village: have brownish color; if from city: gold ➕
- Become permanently tired after 48 days ➕
- Age and die after 50 days (`Aging` mixin) ➕
- Don't drop blessings when dying of old age ➕
- Can carry up to 3 blessings that they sell off in the first city they visit ❌
- Won't take more blessings than they can carry ❌
- Have a rotation of daily moods ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Mercenary ➕

- Escorts a caravan ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Tired ➕

- If not part of a party: occurs after three consecutive non-tired days ➕
- Protects the settlement they are in ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Adventurous ➕

- Travels to two different remote settlements in a day ➕
- Visits other settlements on the way ➕
- Occasionally explores the area ➕
- Remembers nearby dragon lairs ➕
- Bands up with other heroes if any of them remember a domain ➕
- Rushes to protect nearby caravans and settlements from attacks ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Opportunistic ➕

- Occurs when a memory of a domain persists for 10 days ➕
- Attempts to rob the domain when the dragon is missing; hero dies if caught alone ➕
- Steals 1 blessing if successful ➕
- Alternatively, occurs the next day after seeing ruins or treasury that hold a blessing ➕
- Ruins and treasury pillaging takes priority over domain robbery ➕
- If pillaging ruins or treasury, takes all the blessings they can ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Vengeful ➕

- Occurs after a hero learns that a hero they know is killed ➕
- Travels to two different remote settlements in a day ➕
- Ignores other settlements, but often explores the area looking for bandits ➕
- Rushes to protect nearby caravans and settlements from attacks ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Foreboding ➕

- Occurs the morning when the party is full ➕
- Mood of the party leader as they lead the attack on the domain ➕

#### Heroes - Mood: Subservient ➕

- Mood of the non-leader party members ➕

#### Heroes - Party Mechanics ➕

- An incomplete hero party chooses the leader at random and follows them ➕
- The leader acts as a normal hero until a full hero party of 4 is assembled ➕
- Heroes disband after raiding the domain and become tired; they don't become vengeful about their party members ➕
- Heroes can become acquaintances after some shared activities ➕
  - Spending time in the same settlement ➕
  - Protecting or escorting the same people 〰
  - Becoming party members ➕
  - Pillaging the same domain, treasury or ruins 〰

### Dragons 〰

- When thinking or being talked about, use pronouns defined by user (ex. he/him/his, ae/aer/aers); default to it/it/its ➕
- Appear at a random tile that matches the dragon type and is far from other lairs ❌
- Scorched dragons can appear on any tile type ❌
- A domain is created at the spawn point ➕
- Scorched lairs project circle radius 10 with terrain changes and background color ➕
  - Water → cracked bed (ʬ), grass → ash (…), forest → burnt (ɹ/ɺ), mountains → darker ➕
  - Scorched land is considered plains for pathfinding purposes (except mountains) ❌
  - Background color #1a0000 (dark red) ➕
  - Removed when dragon is dead; effects reversed ➕
- Type determines symbol/rotation: serpent, brute, blade, druid, midas ➕
- Domain determines color: aquatic (#004080), mountain (#808080), verdant (#008000), flame (#800000) ➕
- Bresenham-style line movement with error accumulation ➕
- Circle around their target ➕
- Age and die after 20 days + 5 days per existing spire (`Aging` mixin with dynamic lifespan) ➕
- Carnivores feed on cattle or fish ➕
- Herbivores occupy grassland to graze ➕
- Greedy dragons don't feed ➕
- Anthropophages seek out people to eat ➕

#### Dragon Types ➕

- **Serpent:** Circle targets in an 8 shape and disorient everyone in a radius when not fighting 〰
- **Blade:** Ignore diagonal movement penalty 〰
  - Can't be defended against ❌
- **Brute:** Reduce settlement health to 1 after a single attack, and immediately destroy camps ➕
- **Druid:** Tend to all spirits in radius 8 rather than single target ➕
- **Midas:** Can accumulate up to 10 blessings in the domain ➕
- **Fragile:** May shed a blessing after fights near where the fight took place ❌

#### Dragon Behaviors ➕

- Dragons can tend to hoard, creating a single blessing per day and storing up to 5 in the domain ➕
- Dragons will tend to hoard even if they already have 5 blessings ➕
- Dragons can tend a spirit that doesn't have a blessing, bestowing a single blessing ➕
- Have a rotation of daily moods ➕

#### Dragon - Mood: Dreary ➕

- Tends to hoard ➕
- If not good: attacks nearby humans ➕
- If evil: attacks a settlement ➕

#### Dragon - Mood: Inspired ➕

- Tends to hoard ➕
- Travels to two spirits far from its domain to tend them ➕

#### Dragon - Mood: Pensive ➕

- Feeds once (tends to hoard if greedy) ➕
- Travels to a spirit close to its domain to tend it ➕

#### Dragon - Mood: Hungry ➕

- Occurs every three days ➕
- Feeds, then rests, then feeds again ➕
- If anthropophage: feeds only once; may be sated by simply attacking (territorial dragon protecting its domain, good dragon protecting caravan from a bandit) ➕
- If greedy: becomes covetous instead ➕
- If evil: attacks random human entity instead of resting ➕

#### Dragon - Mood: Covetous ➕

- Attacks a settlement and steals a blessing ➕
- If good: becomes inspired instead ➕

#### Dragon - General Combat

- When territorial dragons are near their domain, they always attack nearby humans ➕
- A good dragon rushes to protect nearby humans that are attacked ➕
- An evil dragon attacks humans that are near the spirit the dragon currently tends ➕
- After death, domain becomes pillage-able treasury ➕

### Blessings ➕

- Can be carried by a caravan one at a time ➕
- Can be picked up and carried by a hero or a bandit up to three at a time ➕
- Dropped on ground on death; persist until picked up ➕
- Also dropped on ground after camp death ➕
- Can be pillaged from ruins, lairs and treasuries ➕

## Damage Values ➕

- Dragons destroy unprotected caravans and kill bandits ➕
- Dragons deal 1 damage to unprotected settlements ➕
- Bandits deal 1 damage to unprotected settlements, but don't trigger mourning ➕
- Fighting a dragon makes heroes tired, unless the hero is vengeful ➕
- Dragons kill tired heroes that are not in a settlement ➕
- Vengeful heroes kill bandits ➕
- Party of heroes kills a dragon, but one (two against blade dragons) of the heroes must die ➕
